This policy applies to personal information that OpenCI, Inc. (“OpenCI”, “we” or “us”) handles when you use our website, dashboard and CI/CD service.

## Your account and Google sign-in

When you select **Continue with Google**, Google provides an account identifier, your email address and basic profile information such as your name and profile picture, when available. We store these details with your OpenCI Cloud account in Firebase Authentication. We use them to sign you in, associate your activity with your account and identify you to members of teams you join. Your teammates can see your name, email address and profile picture in the member list.

## Repositories, builds and integrations

When you connect a repository, we process repository and commit identifiers, source code, workflow configuration and the credentials you authorize us to use. We use them to run the builds, tests and deployments you request. Your team's members can access its build results and logs. A workflow may send build status, artifacts or release information to destinations your team selects, such as GitHub or an app distribution service.

We use access controls to restrict team data and a managed secret store for credentials registered as OpenCI Cloud secrets. When you connect to your own Firebase project or runner, the operator of that environment controls access to the information processed there.

## Usage, diagnostics and support

We use build times, job results and usage records to operate runners, calculate charges and investigate failures. We may collect IP addresses, device and browser information, and diagnostic events to keep the service secure and improve its reliability. When dashboard diagnostics are enabled, Sentry may receive crash reports, performance data, screenshots and session replays. These may include information visible on screen.

The website and dashboard use browser storage or cookies for functions such as keeping you signed in and remembering your language or display settings. You can manage them through your browser. If you contact support, we use the information you provide to respond and to send notices about your account or the service.

## Payments

For web billing, Stripe collects the payment details you enter. We use customer and payment-method identifiers, invoices, payment status and team usage to bill the team, answer inquiries and keep accounting records. For App Store purchases, Apple handles the payment. Where in-app purchases use RevenueCat, we use your OpenCI account identifier and purchase or subscription status to enable the features you purchased.

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We share it with service providers as needed for the functions described above, with teammates according to their access, and with integrations you choose. We may also disclose it when required by law, to protect the rights or safety of users or others, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and for purposes such as accounting, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. You can delete your sign-in account in the dashboard settings. Account deletion does not automatically erase shared team build records or invoices that we still need for these purposes.

## Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of use or data portability, object to processing, or withdraw consent you have given. For these requests, or to ask about how we protect your data, contact [support@openci.org](mailto:support@openci.org). We may ask for information needed to verify your identity.
